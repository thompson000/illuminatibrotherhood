₿ Payments & Crypto
Why does the Brotherhood accept Bitcoin?▾
The Brotherhood values absolute discretion in all transactions. Bitcoin and other cryptocurrencies allow initiates to acquire sacred artifacts without creating a traceable financial record. This protects both buyer and Brotherhood alike. Crypto payments are confirmed after 1–3 blockchain confirmations.
Which cryptocurrencies are accepted?▾
We accept Bitcoin (BTC), Ethereum (ETH), and USDT (TRC-20 network). Crypto payments are processed at the exchange rate at time of order. You will receive a Brotherhood wallet address valid for 60 minutes per order to ensure accurate conversion rates.
Is PayPal or card payment safe?▾
Yes. Card and PayPal transactions are processed through our encrypted payment gateway using 256-bit SSL encryption. For maximum discretion, we recommend cryptocurrency. Card payments may appear on statements under a neutral merchant name unrelated to the Brotherhood.
What if my crypto payment hasn't confirmed?▾
Blockchain confirmation typically takes 10–60 minutes depending on network congestion. If your transaction has not confirmed after 2 hours, contact support with your transaction hash. Do not resend — duplicate payments will be returned minus network fees.
🔺 Initiation & Membership
How does Brotherhood initiation work?▾
Initiation is not applied for — it is extended by invitation. The Brotherhood observes individuals of exceptional intellect, character, and discipline over time. If you are deemed ready, you will be contacted through channels you will recognise. Purchasing artifacts is one way to demonstrate your seriousness and alignment with Brotherhood values.
Can I request membership directly?▾
You may submit an expression of interest via our contact form below. Include your background, why you seek illumination, and what you offer to the Brotherhood. All submissions are reviewed by the Council. Most receive no response — not from rejection, but because the decision is not yet made. Patience is itself a test.
What degrees of membership exist?▾
The Brotherhood operates on a 33-degree system of initiation. Initiates begin at the 1st degree (Entered Apprentice) and progress through demonstrated mastery of each level's teachings. The highest 3 degrees (31st–33rd) are invitation-only and their knowledge is never written. Progression is entirely merit-based.
💍 Artifacts & Items
Are Brotherhood artifacts genuinely sacred?▾
Brotherhood artifacts are crafted by master artisans working under specific astronomical and ceremonial conditions. Crystals and metals are selected from verified sources of ancient provenance. Rings are engraved with the buyer's initiate cipher after consecration. Whether you believe in their metaphysical properties is secondary — their craftsmanship and rarity are objectively unmatched.
What materials are used in Brotherhood rings?▾
Standard Brotherhood rings are crafted from 18k gold with inlay options including obsidian, onyx, ruby, and sapphire. The Grand Master ring uses black gold — a rare alloy used exclusively by top-tier jewelers. All rings arrive with a Brotherhood material certification sealed in wax.
How does the Charisma Amulet work?▾
The Charisma Amulet functions through a combination of symbolic anchoring (a psychological principle proven in behavioural science) and the rare copper-mineral matrix it is composed of. Brotherhood wearers report measurable improvements in social confidence, persuasive authority, and interpersonal magnetism. Wear it close to the sternum for optimal effect.
🔐 Security & Privacy
Is my personal data kept private?▾
The Brotherhood does not maintain customer databases in any publicly accessible system. Your delivery address is encrypted, used once, and purged from our records after courier handoff. We do not sell, share, or monetise any initiate information under any circumstances.
How do I know this site is legitimate?▾
The Brotherhood has no need for external validation. Our legitimacy is demonstrated through the centuries — not through certificates or reviews. That said: our artifacts are physical goods, our couriers are real, and our craftsmen are verifiable. First-time buyers may request a sample correspondence via encrypted email before purchase.
