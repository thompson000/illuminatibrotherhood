What We Seek

Those Who Are Called

The Brotherhood evaluates candidates by their conviction, character, and willingness to carry the burden of secrecy. We welcome those who honour tradition, seek truth, and are prepared to shoulder responsibility.

If you are ready to join our order, complete the application below. Each submission is reviewed by council, and only those aligned with the path will be invited further.

  • • A clear purpose for seeking membership
  • • Respect for tradition and discretion
  • • A willingness to learn sacred symbols and rituals
